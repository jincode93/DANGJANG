- Fabric stretch and fit intent (B7: an oversized product gets recommended one size down).

**R1 auto-decision: narrow the claim.** Say DANGJANG fixes part-level dimension failures. Name 어울림·색감 as the other half of the problem and out of scope. **Do-nothing cost.** Users keep failing quietly; the platform never sees it in return stats. That is the strongest part of the case and also the hardest to prove to a host platform (see Layer 3 above).
